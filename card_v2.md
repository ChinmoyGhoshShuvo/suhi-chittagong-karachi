**Title:** Surface Urban Heat Islands in Two Coastal Megacities: Chittagong vs Karachi

**Summary:** An automated Google Earth Engine workflow tracks surface urban heat island intensity from 2000 to 2024 in a humid (Chittagong) and an arid (Karachi) coastal megacity. It uses MODIS land surface temperature, land cover and sea-level-rise exposure. The work has been presented at a conference and a manuscript is in preparation; results will be shared after publication.
